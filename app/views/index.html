Dynamics 365 and Power Pages manual

Principles, guidance and standards for building joined-up, user-centred DfE services on Dynamics 365 and Power Pages, based on GOV.UK standards.

About this manual

This manual provides standards, patterns and guidance for teams building services on Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Power Pages within the Department for Education.

It supports the DfE Customer Relationship Management standard (DDTS-760) (opens in a new tab), which establishes Dynamics 365 as the approved CRM platform for contact centre operations, grant management, data collection, and programme management across the department.

The guidance helps make sure Dynamics 365 and Power Pages services are consistent, accessible and aligned with Government Digital Service (GDS) standards, whether you're building a public-facing Power Pages website or configuring the back-end CRM.

What's included

  • Power Pages

    Build accessible websites that follow the GOV.UK Design System, using custom web templates and the CRM helper framework.

  • Dynamics 365 CRM

    CRM

Why this exists

Microsoft's out-of-the-box Power Pages features produce HTML that teams cannot fully control, so they cannot guarantee it meets government accessibility standards. There was also no consistent approach to building or configuring Dynamics 365 services across DfE.

This manual was created to solve that problem. It provides:

Power Pages

  • Base website templates to import into your environment, built with GOV.UK Frontend instead of Microsoft's default styling
  • Page templates for consistent layouts, headings and navigation
  • The CRM helper framework, for validating forms and saving data to CRM through the Power Pages Web API
  • GOV.UK components adapted for Power Pages, plus components for needs the GOV.UK Design System does not cover
  • Guidance on server logic, for work that has to run on the server, such as scanning uploaded files for viruses

Dynamics 365 CRM

  • A consistent way of building Dynamics 365 solutions
  • Clear guidance on choosing the right level of customisation
  • Reusable patterns, components and solution structures that help create maintainable and scalable solutions
  • A common approach to security, environments and deployments, ensuring compliant and well-governed practices

The aim is to help teams deliver user-centred, accessible services on Dynamics 365 and Power Pages without having to solve the same problems repeatedly.
