The main question is whether users need to work with restricted tables, such as Case. If they create, update or own cases, they need a Dynamics 365 Customer Service Enterprise licence.

Which licence to use

Which licence to use
Licence Use it for
Customer Service Enterprise The standard licence for most operational teams at DfE. Users who manage cases, or use queues, knowledge articles, SLAs, routing or the Copilot Service workspace.
Sales Enterprise Users who need sales features, such as leads, opportunities, quotes and orders. It does not give full access to cases.
Team Member Users who mainly view records, reports and dashboards, and make light updates to data that isn't restricted.
Power Apps Users of custom model-driven apps that only use custom tables, or standard tables that aren't restricted, such as Account and Contact. It can be cheaper, but it doesn't cover restricted tables, sales features or the Dynamics 365 apps.

Power Automate flows are covered by DfE's Dynamics 365 licences when they use Dynamics 365 data. A flow that doesn't may need its own licence.
