Products and how DfE uses them

Dynamics 365 and Power Platform products used at DfE
Product How DfE uses it
Dynamics 365 Customer Service (opens in a new tab) The main CRM application across DfE. Use it for services that manage contacts, organisations, enquiries and cases.
Dynamics 365 Sales (opens in a new tab) Used by one CRM to track leads and opportunities with employers. Only use it where a service needs a sales pipeline.
Dynamics 365 Customer Insights (opens in a new tab) Used by one CRM to send bulk email. Only use it where a service needs marketing journeys or bulk email.
Power Apps (opens in a new tab) Model-driven apps are the default for internal users. Dynamics 365 applications are model-driven apps with pre-built functionality. Use a canvas app only for a small, task-specific app, or one that needs data from outside Dataverse.
Power Pages (opens in a new tab) Used for all external-facing services that sit on a CRM. Build them with the DfE Power Pages framework, with sign-in through DfE Sign-in or GOV.UK One Login, and follow the shared patterns and components.
Power Automate (opens in a new tab) Used across all DfE CRMs for automation and lightweight integration. See when to use Power Automate.
Power BI (opens in a new tab) The standard tool for reporting beyond what views, charts and dashboards provide. See reporting and analytics.
Dataverse (opens in a new tab) The data platform under every DfE CRM. Design tables to the table and column standards.
