Use teams to give groups of users shared access and shared ownership of records, without changing the business unit structure. Microsoft explains the types of team (opens in a new tab) and Microsoft Entra group teams (opens in a new tab).

Which type of team to use

When to use each type of team
Team type Use it for
Owner team The default. Teams that share work or own records together, such as a team that owns its cases. Members get the team's security roles. You manage membership in the CRM.
Microsoft Entra group team Large groups, or groups whose members change often. You create the team in the environment and link it to a Microsoft Entra security group or Microsoft 365 group. Membership comes from the group, so it's managed outside the CRM, usually through a service request.
Access team Temporary or ad hoc collaboration on individual records. Access teams don't own records or have security roles.

Do

  • Assign security roles to teams rather than to individual users, wherever possible.
  • Reuse existing teams before creating new ones.
  • Let Microsoft Entra group teams sync their membership, rather than adding people by hand.
  • Remember that a team's roles give real access to everyone in it, so assign them carefully.

Don't

  • Don't create a team for every permission scenario.
  • Don't use access teams for access across the system. They only apply to individual records.
  • Don't use teams to patch over a poorly designed business unit structure.
