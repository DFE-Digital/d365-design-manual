The standards DfE teams follow when they design, build, deploy and secure Dynamics 365 CRM services.

Dynamics 365 offers many ways to solve the same problem, through configuration, low-code tools and custom code. Without shared standards, teams solve the same problems in different ways, and services become harder to support. These standards help DfE CRMs stay consistent, reusable and supportable, in line with the CRM strategy.

The manual sets standards and links to Microsoft's documentation for how each feature works.

Who it's for

It's for people who build or extend Dynamics 365 and Power Platform solutions, and who already know Dataverse, solutions, source control and Azure DevOps pipelines.
