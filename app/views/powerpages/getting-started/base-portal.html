Every DfE Power Pages site starts from the base portal, which the guidance in this manual is written for.

The base portal includes:

  • GOV.UK Frontend and DfE Frontend styles
  • the page templates for common layouts
  • the CRM helper framework for validating forms and saving data to CRM

Get a copy

To get a copy of the base portal, email the Solutions Delivery Team at solutionsdevelopment.techdirectorate@education.gov.uk. Tell them which branding your service needs.

Before you install it, complete the pre-requisites in your Dynamics 365 environment.
